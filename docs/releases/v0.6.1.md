# v0.6.1

## SafeNet Fusion PIV reset

`piv setup reset` explicitly clears the Card Authentication slot (`9E`) when
the PC/SC reader identifies a SafeNet Fusion. It clears the key container and
public mirror even when the card omits their tags from its discovery lists. A
rejected `9E` cleanup command stops the reset.

SafeNet reset requires the current management key and PUK. It rejects an empty
PUK or one longer than eight bytes before sending reset commands. The card
checks the PUK when restoring the factory PIN after cleanup.

On a physical Fusion, a full reset followed by reconnection made the old keys
in `9A`, `9C`, `9D`, and `9E` unusable for signing. The factory PIN, PUK, and
management key were also verified. [Hardware verification](https://github.com/PeculiarVentures/piv-go/pull/27#issuecomment-7158234264)

## Factory credentials in the CLI

After a successful full PIV reset on SafeNet or YubiKey, the CLI's
human-readable output shows the restored PIN, PUK, and management key. It
includes the SafeNet management key algorithm. The YubiKey factory management
algorithm depends on firmware.

Go adapters can provide these values through the optional
`adapters.ResetDefaultsAdapter` interface. Existing callers can continue to
use `adapters.ResetRequirements` as before.
